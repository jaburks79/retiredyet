Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Retired-Yet.com ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website retired-yet.com. This page explains how we handle information when you use our free retirement countdown timer.

Information We Collect

We do not collect or store any personal information on our servers. The name, retirement date, and salary you enter are saved only in your browser's local storage on your own device — they are never transmitted to us or any third party.

Local Storage

If you check "Remember my retirement date on this device," your details are saved in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device. You can clear it at any time by unchecking the box and clicking Start countdown, or by clearing your browser's site data.

Cookies and Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

We also use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. Google Analytics collects anonymized usage data. No personally identifiable information is collected. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdSense — advertising. Privacy policy
  • Google Analytics — anonymous site usage analytics. Privacy policy
  • Google Fonts — typography. Privacy policy

Children's Privacy

Retired-Yet.com is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
